# One logical playback session across feed and full series

Native TTVideoEngine instances remain under the official app. The module does not create or merge decoders. On an explicit player-page launch or a different actual play command, retire the former mini session, restore its renderer and send pause to that exact previous engine. Guard briefly against retired autoplay, and allow mini-issued pause through system-media pause protection. Renderer selection must belong to the current Activity.

Full series now has a dedicated minimize button and a narrowly scoped legacy Back hook. A live Main Activity in the same task is required. REORDER_TO_FRONT returns to it without calling finish on the series owner; the mini overlay follows to Main. For an explicit expand, return to the retained series Activity. Suppress automatic new play for a short window on returning to Main, not indefinitely. The Home tab must not restore a series-owned mini into the feed. Native predictive Back or toolbar paths that finish the Activity may not hit the legacy hook; the explicit mini button is the reliable entry to test. Surface reattachment, automatic next episode and task history behavior require device verification. No unconditional stop/release hook or access/authentication change.

Regression: feed mini -> series (one audio); series mini button -> Main (series remains); switch tabs and open settings/search; expand to original series; close/pause; start a new series; back gesture vs explicit entry; disable mini; device rotation.
